About Shree Hari Polymers

A plastic waste recycling business specialising in PET.

Who we are

Shree Hari Polymers is a plastic waste recycling business located at Site-4, Sahibabad Industrial Area, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh. We specialise in PET (polyethylene terephthalate) plastic and provide sustainable raw material solutions for the fibre, sheet, chemical and monofilament industries.

What we do

  • PET plastic recycling: converting PET plastic waste into usable raw material in the form of recycled PET flakes.
  • Custom solutions: recycled material supplied to suit the needs of fibre, sheet and monofilament producers.
  • Waste collection: collection and processing of plastic waste to minimise its environmental impact.

Our purpose

Our aim is simple: transform plastic waste into solutions. Every PET bottle that is recycled into flakes is kept out of landfill and replaces virgin plastic made from fossil resources. By supplying dependable recycled raw material, we help Indian manufacturers reduce both cost and environmental impact.
