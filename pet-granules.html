PET Granules: Recycled PET Granules Explained

PET granules, also called rPET granules, PET chips or PET pellets, are uniform pellets made by melting and filtering recycled PET flakes. This guide covers how they are made, their grades and uses, and when flakes are the better buy.

Send a requirement See PET flakes

What are PET granules?

PET granules are small, uniform pellets of polyethylene terephthalate, usually 2–5 mm in size. Recycled PET granules (rPET granules) are made from washed PET bottle flakes or clean PET industrial scrap. The material is melted in an extruder, passed through fine melt filters, cut into pellets and often crystallised. Virgin PET granules (“PET chips” or “PET resin”) are made directly from petrochemicals.

How PET granules are made from scrap

  1. PET scrap collection: bottle scrap and sheet scrap are bought and sorted.
  2. Flaking and washing: material is ground, hot washed and dried into PET flakes.
  3. Extrusion and filtration: flakes are dried and melted, and the melt is filtered to remove fine contamination.
  4. Pelletising: the strands are cut into granules, then crystallised and dried for storage.
  5. Optional SSP: solid-state polycondensation raises the intrinsic viscosity (IV) for bottle-grade or strapping-grade uses.

PET flakes vs PET granules

PET flakesPET granules
FormIrregular pieces, about 8–14 mmUniform pellets, about 2–5 mm
ProcessingWashed and driedWashed, melted, filtered and pelletised
Bulk densityLower (about 250–400 kg/m³)Higher (about 750–850 kg/m³)
FeedingNeeds suitable feedersFeeds like virgin resin
CostLowerHigher, because of the extra extrusion step
Common usersPolyester staple fibre, sheet, strapping makers with their own extrusionFilament yarn, injection moulding, sheet and film lines that need uniform feed

Grades and uses of PET granules

  • Fibre grade: polyester staple fibre and filament yarn
  • Sheet and film grade: thermoforming sheet, blister and packaging film
  • Strapping grade: high-IV material for PET strapping
  • Injection and engineering grade: compounded parts and moulded items
  • Bottle grade (food contact): needs certified decontamination processes and regulatory approval

Frequently asked questions

What are PET granules?

PET granules are uniform pellets of PET plastic, about 2-5 mm in size. Recycled PET granules (rPET) are made by melting, filtering and pelletising washed PET flakes.

What is the difference between PET flakes and PET granules?

Flakes are washed, ground bottle pieces. Granules are flakes that have been melted, filtered and cut into uniform pellets. Granules feed more easily but cost more.

What are recycled PET granules used for?

Polyester fibre and filament yarn, PET sheet and film, strapping, injection moulded parts and, with certified processes, food-grade bottles.
