Recycled PET Flakes

PET bottle flakes supplied as an industrial raw material for fibre, sheet, chemical and monofilament production. Here is what to know before you buy.

What are PET flakes?

PET flakes are the product of mechanically recycling PET (polyethylene terephthalate) bottles. Used bottles are sorted, stripped of caps and labels, ground into small pieces, washed and dried. The result is a free-flowing flake that can be melted and re-formed into new products. You will also see them called PET bottle flakes, rPET flakes or recycled PET flakes.

Types of PET flakes

By colour

  • Clear (natural / transparent) PET flakes — made from colourless water and beverage bottles. The most versatile and most sought-after grade because it can be used for white fibre and clear sheet.
  • Light blue PET flakes — from lightly tinted water bottles; often blended with clear flakes.
  • Green PET flakes — from green soft-drink bottles; used for coloured fibre, strapping and monofilament.
  • Brown / amber and mixed colour PET flakes — used where colour does not matter, such as dark fibre, strapping and some chemical uses.

By washing method

  • Hot washed PET flakes — washed in hot water with caustic soda and detergent, which removes glue, oil and fine dirt. Preferred for fibre, sheet and any application sensitive to contamination.
  • Cold washed PET flakes — washed in ambient water only. Lower cost, with more residual glue and dirt; usually washed again by the buyer or used in less demanding applications.
  • Unwashed (dry ground) PET flakes — bottles that have only been ground. Sold to recyclers who run their own washing line.

How to choose the right PET flakes

  1. Start with your end product. White or dyed fibre and clear sheet need clear, hot washed flakes with very low PVC. Strapping, dark fibre and monofilament can accept coloured flakes.
  2. Ask for the contamination figures. PVC, polyolefin and moisture levels decide how well the material runs on your line.
  3. Check consistency, not just one sample. A reliable supplier delivers the same quality lot after lot.
  4. Agree packing and quantity. Flakes are normally shipped in jumbo bags; confirm bag weight and load size.

PET flakes vs PET chips and granules

Flakes are the direct output of washing and grinding bottles. PET chips or rPET granules are made by melting flakes, filtering the melt and cutting it into uniform pellets. Granules are more uniform and easier to feed but cost more. Many fibre and sheet producers run directly on good-quality flakes.
