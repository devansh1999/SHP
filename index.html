PET flakes from a dedicated PET recycler

PET (polyethylene terephthalate) is the plastic used for water, soft-drink and edible-oil bottles, and it is one of the most widely recycled plastics in India. When used bottles are sorted, crushed, washed and dried they become PET flakes — also called PET bottle flakes or rPET flakes — which manufacturers use in place of virgin polymer.

Why manufacturers use recycled PET flakes

Lower raw material cost

Recycled PET flakes are generally priced below virgin PET resin, which helps manufacturers manage input costs.

Lower environmental footprint

Producing rPET uses less energy than making virgin PET from petrochemicals and keeps bottles out of landfill and waterways.

Recycled-content compliance

India’s Plastic Waste Management Rules and Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) framework are pushing brands towards recycled content.

Common questions

What are PET flakes?

PET flakes are small pieces of PET plastic, typically 8–14 mm across, made by sorting, grinding, washing and drying used PET bottles. They are sold as a recycled raw material.

What are PET flakes used for?

Mostly polyester staple fibre, followed by PET sheet, strapping, monofilament and chemical applications. See uses of PET flakes.
