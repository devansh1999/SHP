What decides the rate of PET scrap?

The price of PET scrap changes with demand from fibre and sheet makers, so we quote per lot. These are the main factors that move the rate up or down:

FactorGets a better rateLowers the rate
ColourClear / transparent, sorted by colourMixed colours, dark or opaque PET
CleanlinessDry, little dirt, no food or oilMud, sand, oil, liquid left in bottles
Other plasticsOnly PET in the loadPVC, PP, HDPE, PS mixed in
Caps and labelsFew caps, removable labelsFull-body PVC sleeves, heavy glue
FormTight bales, consistent loadsLoose, mixed, inconsistent loads
QuantityFull loads and regular monthly supplySmall, one-time lots

What we do not buy

  • PVC sheet or bottles, PS/HIPS, PP and other non-PET plastics sold as PET
  • Medical, hazardous or chemical-contaminated waste
  • Wet loads, or material with stones, metal or sand added to raise weight
  • Small household quantities

Documents

We are GST registered (GSTIN 09CBOPA7522G1ZM). Sellers should provide a GST invoice and an e-way bill where applicable. Recycled PET also counts towards producers’ obligations under India’s Plastic Waste Management Rules and Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) framework. Ask us if you need recycling documentation.
