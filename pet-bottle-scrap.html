What counts as PET bottle scrap?

PET bottle scrap is used bottles made from PET (polyethylene terephthalate), marked with resin code 1 or “PET/PETE”. In India most of it comes from packaged drinking water, soft-drink, juice and edible-oil bottles. In the scrap trade it is also called PET bottle kabad, pet bottle bhangar or simply PET scrap.

How to get a better rate for PET bottle scrap

  1. Sort by colour. Clear bottles kept apart from coloured ones get a higher rate.
  2. Keep it dry and empty. Water, sand and liquid inside bottles add weight that is deducted.
  3. Remove non-PET items. Glass, metal, PVC bottles, HDPE containers and polythene lower the value of the whole load.
  4. Bale tightly. Dense bales cut transport cost per kg, which leaves more room in the rate.
  5. Supply regularly. Steady monthly loads are worth more to a recycler than one-off lots.

What happens to your bottles

At our unit the bales are opened, sorted, de-capped and de-labelled, ground into flakes, washed and dried. The resulting recycled PET flakes go to polyester fibre, PET sheet, strapping and monofilament manufacturers. See the full PET recycling process.
