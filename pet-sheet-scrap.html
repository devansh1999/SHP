What is PET sheet scrap?

PET sheet scrap is waste from making or converting PET (APET or RPET) sheet. Thermoforming lines that make food trays, clamshells, cups, lids, blisters and boxes leave behind large amounts of PET sheet cutting: the skeleton left after parts are punched out, plus edge trims and start-up waste. This clean, single-polymer industrial scrap is good feedstock for recycling.

Please declare these before sending

  • PVC sheet looks very similar to PET but contaminates PET recycling. Do not mix it in.
  • Laminated or coated sheet (PE-laminated, metallised, heavily printed or anti-fog coated)
  • Sheet with protective film still attached
  • PS, HIPS or PP sheet, which is not PET

How to tell PET sheet from PVC sheet

  • Check the code: PET carries resin code 1 (PET/PETE); PVC carries code 3 (V/PVC).
  • Bend test: PVC usually shows a white stress mark on a sharp fold; clear PET mostly does not.
  • Burn test (done safely, outdoors): PET burns with a yellow, slightly smoky flame and drips. PVC is hard to keep burning, gives a green-edged flame and a sharp, acidic smell.
  • If in doubt, send us a sample or a photo and the source of the material.
